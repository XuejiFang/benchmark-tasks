# Source dishwasher asset

LG Electronics authored `dishwasher_ldfn4542b` in its SimReady asset collection. This folder redistributes the source USD and textures under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). [Pinned LG Electronics dataset asset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/LGElectronics/simready-assets/tree/96684f4c9c6205aef8f53abc9a09ce0f49234507/dishwasher_ldfn4542b/simready_usd), [dataset license declaration](https://huggingface.co/datasets/LGElectronics/simready-assets/blob/96684f4c9c6205aef8f53abc9a09ce0f49234507/README.md).

The three bundled OmniPBR material files carry NVIDIA's BSD-3-Clause notice. The original headers and source bytes are retained. [BSD-3-Clause license text](LICENSE-NVIDIA-MDL.txt).

At runtime, the fixture positions the appliance, anchors its base, starts its door at the horizontal stop and adds a visible orange grip to the upper rack. The source USD and texture bytes under `assets/dishwasher` are preserved. The three test cups are task assets.
